Guide A & B • Geographical Itinerary

San Lázaro Archipelago Route

  • San Lázaro Archipelago (Archipiélago de San Lázaro): Named after the Sunday of St. Lazarus (March 17, 1521). Coordinates recorded at $10^\circ\text{ N}$ latitude, $161^\circ$ from the Tordesillas Line of Demarcation.
  • Samar (Zamal): Spotted March 16 as "high land", 300 leagues (1,666.8 km) from Mariana Islands.
  • Homonhon (Humunu): Uninhabited landing spot on March 17. Named Aguada de los Buenos Indicios ("Watering Place of Good Signs") due to freshwater springs and gold traces.
  • Suluan (Zuloan): Home of the first local inhabitants encountered by the fleet.
  • Southwestern Course Waypoints: Leyte (Cenalo/Ceylon), Hinunangan (Hiunanghan - mistaken as an island), Hibusong (Ibusson), and San Juan (Abarien).

Guide B • Limasawa & Camotes Waypoints

Limasawa (Mazaua) to Cebu Passage

  • Limasawa (Mazaua): Spotted March 27 via night fires. Situated at $9\frac{2}{3}^\circ\text{ N}$ latitude, 25 leagues (138.9 km) from Homonhon. Hunting ground of Rajah Colambu and site of the First Mass.
  • Intervening Waypoints to Cebu: Canigao (Canigan), Baybay (coastal city mistaken as an island), and Himokilan (Gatighan - 111.12 km from Limasawa).
  • Fauna documented at Himokilan: Eagle-sized bats (tasted like chicken), black birds with goose-sized eggs, doves, and parrots.
  • Camotes Islands: Polo, Pacijan (Ticobon), and Ponson (Pozon) where the fleet waited for Rajah Colambu's boats.

Guide A & C • Historical Value

Pigafetta’s Log & 1982 Spring Rediscovery

Pigafetta recorded specific natural resources: freshwater springs, gold, white coral, and palm trees. In 1982, researchers cross-referenced his descriptions to locate the exact freshwater springs at Homonhon lost for over 450 years.

Guide A • Suluan Contact Details

First Encounters at Homonhon

  • Locals from Suluan met the fleet at Homonhon. Magellan presented red caps, mirrors, combs, bells, and ivory objects.
  • Locals presented Uraca (palm wine), bananas, and coconuts. Pigafetta noted detailed accounts of native coconut harvesting.
  • Locals returned 4 days later with live chickens, oranges, and additional coconuts.

Guide B • Political Networks

Sanduguan & Pre-Colonial Alliances

Magellan met Rajah Colambu of Limasawa and his brother Rajah Siaiu of Butuan. The sides sealed a blood compact (Sanduguan). Magellan declined a large gold bar offer, establishing mutual trust.
